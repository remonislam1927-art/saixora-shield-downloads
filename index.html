The idea behind the shield

Every device.
Under the right control.

SAIXORA Shield is a connected SaaS ecosystem that helps organizations and personal users govern supported Windows PCs and Android devices through scheduled access, role-based sessions, remote commands and live visibility.

Discover how the ecosystem works →
The problem

Devices are everywhere.
Control shouldn't be.

When shared devices are left unattended, organizations and families can lose visibility, consistency and accountability.

01

Unapproved access

Unattended smart boards, lab PCs and workstations can be used for unrelated activity or settings changes.

02

No live visibility

Supervisors may not know what a protected screen is showing without visiting the room.

03

Limited accountability

Shared passwords do not provide a clear record of who started a session, for which subject, or when it ended.

04

Manual room operations

Announcements, period changes and end-of-session lockouts can require unnecessary trips and manual follow-up.

Built around the session

From boot-up
to auto-lock.

A protected device can start in a controlled state, open only for an authorised session and return to its locked state when the assigned time ends.

  • Device Shield applies the configured kiosk lock at startup.
  • Staff Shield starts a permitted class, subject or work session.
  • The local session clock continues enforcing expiry during an internet interruption.
  • Admin Shield provides oversight and remote control for authorised administrators.

Capabilities

More than a lock screen.
A live control layer.

Key capabilities described for the SAIXORA Shield ecosystem. Availability and behavior depend on the application release, supported device and configuration.

◈

Remote live view

View an active device screen remotely using a WebRTC-based streaming path. Performance depends on device, network and relay conditions.

◉

Voice & TTS broadcasts

Use push-to-talk voice announcements or typed text-to-speech messages for rooms and connected devices.

▤

Session history

Review active and completed sessions with filters for date range, registered class, subject and period slot.

◫

Screen recording

Where enabled, save an MP4 recording of a monitored session to the administrator's Android device with a visible recording notice.

⌁

Tamper alerts

Receive notifications when the protected endpoint detects configured unauthorised touch or exit attempts.

◷

Scheduled enforcement

Apply session start and end times, period reminders and local-clock expiry enforcement.
