Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy.

Understand how this website is structured and what information must be disclosed before SAIXORA services are launched.

Important publication note: This is a conservative starter notice based on the website code included in this ZIP. It is not a verified privacy policy for the Device Shield, Admin Shield or Staff Shield apps. Before publishing, review the actual app data flows, hosting provider logs, analytics, crash reporting, permissions, SDKs and applicable legal requirements. Add an effective date and privacy contact.

1. Scope

This notice concerns the SAIXORA Technology website. The SAIXORA Shield applications may process information independently of this website. App-specific privacy disclosures must be provided in the apps and/or their official distribution listings based on their real implementation.

2. Information you provide through this website

The contact form in this website template is designed to open a draft message in the visitor's default email application. It does not submit the form to a website server or store the message in this website. When you send the email, your email provider and the recipient's email service will process it under their own policies.

3. Information the SAIXORA Shield service may process

Depending on the application, features enabled and organisation or personal configuration, SAIXORA Shield may process account and organisation identifiers, access-code and subscription status, registered device details, staff or role assignments, session start/end times, class or subject selections, device status and administrative audit events. When live-view, voice, announcement or recording features are used, screen content, audio streams, announcement content or session recordings may also be processed. The exact data categories, recipients, storage locations, retention periods, access roles and service providers must be documented in the final app-specific privacy notice before those features are offered publicly.

Administrators should use screen viewing, voice and recording features only for devices and people they are authorised to manage, and should provide clear notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law and organisational policy. Recording notices should remain visible wherever the product's recording feature is enabled.

4. Technical information

When a website is hosted and accessed, the hosting provider and network infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser details and requested resources in server or security logs. The exact information, retention period and legal basis depend on the hosting provider and hosting configuration. Confirm these details with your provider before publication.

5. Cookies and analytics

The website source in this package does not intentionally include analytics, advertising trackers or optional cookie-based measurement. Hosting infrastructure, embedded third-party content or services added later may behave differently. Update this policy before adding analytics, embedded media, advertising or other third-party scripts.

6. Links to other websites

Links to the founder's portfolio or other external websites are provided for convenience. We do not control their privacy practices. Read the relevant privacy notice before sharing information with an external service.

7. How long information is kept

This website template does not maintain a server-side contact database. Email correspondence, if you choose to send it, may be retained by the email systems involved and by the recipient according to their practices and applicable requirements. Hosting log retention must be confirmed with the host.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other treatment of personal information. The proper contact route and response process must be supplied by SAIXORA Technology before publication.

9. Children's privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If an app or service is intended for children or may be used by them, age-appropriate notices, consent and safeguards must be evaluated separately before release.

10. Changes to this notice

This page should be updated whenever the website, apps, service providers or data practices change. The effective date should reflect the date the approved policy takes effect.

11. Contact

For privacy enquiries, use the Contact page after a monitored privacy contact address has been configured.
