What it is

A connected control layer for your devices.

SAIXORA Shield is a cloud-connected SaaS ecosystem that turns supported Windows PCs and Android devices into scheduled, remotely governed endpoints.

It is designed for environments where a device should only be accessible during an authorised class, subject, work shift or personal-use session — while approved administrators retain visibility and control.

How it works

From a protected device
to an authorised session.

A typical operating flow for a managed endpoint.

01

Register & protect

A supported device is registered and configured with Device Shield. Startup behavior and kiosk restrictions depend on the operating system and device policy.

02

Authorise a session

An authorised staff member selects an assigned device, class, subject and period in Staff Shield. Personal-use configurations can follow their own access rules.

03

Monitor & manage

Administrators use Admin Shield for status, remote commands, available live-view tools, voice announcements and session records.

04

Enforce session expiry

At the configured end time, Device Shield returns the endpoint to its protected state. The local session clock is designed to continue enforcement during internet interruptions.

Technical behavior varies by platform, device permissions, operating-system policy, connectivity and application version. Kiosk controls are designed to resist common exit and tampering paths, but no software should be described as universally impossible to bypass on every device.
Administrator capabilities

Visibility and control, in one place.

◉

Live screen view

View a connected PC or Android screen through WebRTC streaming. The stated design target is up to 24 fps HD where device, network and relay conditions allow.

▣

MP4 recording

Where supported and enabled, record a monitored session to the administrator's Android gallery under Movies/Saixora Shield, with a visible recording notice on the target device.

♫

PTT & TTS

Use push-to-talk voice or a typed text-to-speech announcement to reach connected room speakers, subject to device audio configuration.

▤

Session history

Review active and completed sessions, with filters for date range, registered class, subject and period slot.

◷

Bell & reminders

Schedule audio and visual reminders ahead of a period or session ending.

⌁

Central licensing

Subscription and access-code status are managed centrally. A suspension can revoke further access and trigger connected endpoints to log out and return to their locked state, subject to connectivity and device enforcement rules.
